Scroll-linked background video
==============================
Place your file here as:

  video/bg-scroll.mp4

Recommended:
- 5–15 seconds
- H.264 MP4, 1280x720 or 1920x1080
- Under ~12MB
- Content that looks good scrubbed frame-by-frame
  (slow motion, logo reveal, landscape, particles)

Behavior:
- Scroll down  → video advances (forward)
- Scroll up    → video rewinds (backward)
- Static poster /img/bg-main.jpg shows until video loads
